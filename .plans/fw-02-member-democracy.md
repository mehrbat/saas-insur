# FW-2 — Member democracy

## Context

[Plan 01 §10](01-platform-overview.md#10-future-work-placeholders) scopes member democracy.
D1 gives the committee claim authority and the platform payout execution. D2 gives members
collective ownership of the fund, which the committee manages on their behalf. [Plan 04](04-voting-and-governance-engine.md)
defines committee voting. Cursor `composer-2.5` approved P1 in round 1 on 26 November
2026. The review confirmed the G8 charter and the Phase 3 member voting scope.

## [P1] Adopt a pilot charter and stage member voting

- **Owner and trigger.** Before G8, the product owner drafts a charter with the pilot partner.
  Counsel confirms member rights against the FW-4 ownership and custody structure and FW-6
  committee mandate. The partner and committee approve the charter within their respective authority.
- **Pilot minimum.** Publish the adopted charter before enrolment and record the version each
  member receives. State who manages the fund, what reports members can see, how members can
  raise questions or complaints, and how they receive material-change notices. Define who can
  amend the charter and how member rights are protected pending member-wide voting.
- **Acceptance evidence.** At G8, require the approved charter and a walkthrough in which a
  member can locate fund reports, identify the complaint route in FW-3 and see a material-change
  notice. Trace the roles, rights and amendment authority to the signed FW-4 and FW-6 evidence.
- **Phase 3 trigger.** After the pilot charter and committee authority are settled, open a
  separate design for member-wide approval of major changes, committee elections and removals,
  terms and rotation, delegation, voter eligibility and duplicate-voter prevention. Require
  explicit approval of the decision types that move to members. D1 claim authority remains with
  the committee and claim appeals follow FW-3.
