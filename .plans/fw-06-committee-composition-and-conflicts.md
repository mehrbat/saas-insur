# FW-6 — Live committee composition and conflicts

## Context

Phase 2 G8 placeholder. [Plan 01](01-platform-overview.md#11-decisions-log) assigns claim decisions to the committee, execution to the platform and the fund's benefit to its members. [Plan 04](04-voting-and-governance-engine.md) sets the voting floor. Keep the full composition rules, late-conflict handling and pay policy for this work item before live release.

Australian counsel and the pilot partner must confirm the claims-handling authority and conflict obligations for the proposed roles. [ASIC INFO 253](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-finance-professionals/afs-licensees/apply-for-an-afs-licence/licensing-for-certain-service-providers/claims-handling-and-settling-how-to-comply-with-your-afs-licence-obligations) · [ASIC RG 181](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/find-a-document/regulatory-guides/rg-181-afs-licensing-managing-conflicts-of-interest)

## [P1] Sign off live committee authority and conflict handling

- **Name capacity and interests.** Assign every seat to an identified person and record whom they represent, other roles, whether they are a fund member, and disclosed financial or personal interests. Require declarations at appointment and before a claim vote, plus a route to report a conflict discovered later. Counsel and the partner decide which capacities can sit together.
- **Apply recusals and preserve quorum.** Automatically recuse the claimant's linked user and representatives. Exclude all recused voters from eligibility and require Plan 04's configured quorum and threshold. An approval requires at least two distinct eligible `yes` votes. Resolve a tie, deadlock or loss of quorum through a documented route to an unconflicted, authorised decision body under the same safeguards. Keep the claim pending during escalation. Preserve the notice and review clocks.
- **Record who can act.** The signed authority matrix identifies committee decision powers, platform execution, independent complaint review under FW-3, and shortfall powers under FW-1 and FW-4. If the licensed partner must retain any final claim authority, record the proposed change to D1 and obtain a decision before enabling the live path.
- **Keep incentives independent.** Recommend a fixed term stipend or unpaid role plus documented expenses. Keep compensation independent of claim outcomes, payout size and retained fund surplus under D3. Assign the compensation budget and approval to the pilot charter under FW-2.
- **Gate.** The pilot risk bearer, committee chair and counsel sign the role matrix, conflict and escalation procedure, and compensation terms before G8. Attach a synthetic rehearsal for an own-claim recusal, a late disclosed conflict, an exhausted quorum and a deadlock. Verify there is no unauthorised decision or payout and that each case reaches its authorised handler with a durable record.
