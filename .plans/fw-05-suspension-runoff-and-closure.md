# FW-5 — Suspension, run-off, and closure

## Context

G8 release placeholder. [Plan 01](01-platform-overview.md) sets the lifecycle and member fund
principles. [Plan 02](02-domain-model.md) §§9.1 and 9.10 reserve the transitions and
`lifecycle_change` proposal type. [Plan 05](05-actuarial-engine-and-simulation.md) §24.2
binds legal readiness to the release bundle. Its `runoff_cutoff` is a modelling horizon.

## [P1] Rehearse the pilot continuity and exit process

- Assign activation, delegation and escalation to the risk bearer, committee and platform
  under the signed G8 role matrix. Define who can stop enrolments, lift suspension,
  start run-off or authorise closure, with logged reasons and notices. Agree when
  suspending or resuming new or renewal cover is allowed.
- Maintain acknowledgement, assessment, valid payouts and complaint access after
  suspension or cessation. Route incomplete, reopened and late-reported claims to
  an accountable human. Apply the incident-date cover, applicable reporting terms
  and legal requirements. Keep contact details and the handover route available to members.
- Before closure, require the risk bearer and custodian to reconcile cash, obligations,
  and any transfer of outstanding or incurred-but-not-reported liabilities. Keep
  remaining funds segregated until counsel and the custodian approve treatment under
  member rights and the agreed priority of obligations. Preserve access to required
  audit records and record the authority, amount and recipient of any distribution.

G8 evidence is a rehearsal transcript and a signed operating runbook for the release
bundle, owned by the risk bearer with the platform and reviewed by Australian counsel.
Rehearse an enrolment stop, a pending claim, a claim reported after exit, and blocked
closure with an unresolved liability. Tie funding and reserves to FW-1, complaints to
FW-3, and legal title, insolvency and member rights to FW-4. Confirm alignment with the
insurer's recovery and exit plan where CPS 190 applies. [APRA CPS 190](https://www.apra.gov.au/standards/cps-190)
