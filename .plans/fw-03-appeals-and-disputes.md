# FW-3 — Appeals and disputes

## Context

Phase 2 G8 placeholder for an Australian pilot. [Plan 01](01-platform-overview.md#11-decisions-log)
D1 gives the committee first-instance claim authority, and D4 keeps decisions human. [Plan 02](02-domain-model.md)
reserves appeal states and proposal types. [Plan 08](08-claim-channels-and-mcp.md) governs claim intake.
Cursor `composer-2.5` approved P1 in round 2 on 26 November 2026. The bridge kept P1 open
after round 1 because Cursor returned an extra verdict for an unregistered item.

## [P1] Approve the pilot complaint and review path before G8

- Give members an accessible route for dissatisfaction about a claim (including delay), removal, or
  billing. Capture the first receipt time across supported channels, acknowledge receipt, and assign
  a case linked to the original decision and evidence where applicable.
- Assign an authorised human reviewer independent of the original decision makers. Preserve the
  original record, log the review outcome and reasons, and route any remedy through the authorised
  claim, membership, or payment workflow. Resolve authority conflicts with D1 and FW-6 before G8.
- Require a written outcome explaining the reasons and available escalation. Map complaints to the
  responsible partner or platform operator. Where internal dispute resolution and AFCA apply,
  confirm who owns the response, reporting, deadlines, and AFCA notices. Verify the named firm's
  AFCA membership and product eligibility. Establish a documented external route for other cases.
  [ASIC RG 271](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/find-a-document/regulatory-guides/rg-271-internal-dispute-resolution)
  · [AFCA jurisdiction and powers](https://www.afca.org.au/about-afca/rules-and-guidelines/jurisdiction-and-powers)
- **Gate evidence.** Counsel and the pilot partner approve the owner and escalation matrix by case
  type, response and delay templates, and the applicable deadlines. Rehearse one disputed claim,
  one removal, and one billing case through capture, independent review, notice and remedy or
  external escalation. Keep G8 blocked until sign-off and evidence are recorded.

## Out of scope

Counsel and the partner set detailed deadlines, authority to reopen, remedy rules, and any statutory
reporting duties in the full FW-3 design. Build that design after the pilot's legal structure is chosen.
