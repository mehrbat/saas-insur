# FW-8 - Fraud investigation

## Context

Phase 2 G8 placeholder for the Australian pilot. Plan 01 D1 and D4 reserve claim decisions
for humans under the approved governance model. Plan 06 supplies advisory signals. Plan 02
governs evidence access and retention. FW-3 owns complaints and external recourse.
[Plan 01](01-platform-overview.md#11-decisions-log) · [Plan 06](06-rulebook-and-ai-second-opinion.md) · [Plan 02](02-domain-model.md#123-evidence-access) · [FW-3](fw-03-appeals-and-disputes.md)

## [P1] Approve a pilot fraud referral and independent review process before G8

- Log each referral against its claim with source, reasons, time and evidence references. A named
  human triages relevance and records closure or transfer to an authorised reviewer independent
  of the referral. A signal carries no disposition authority and creates no automatic hold,
  denial, payment interruption or adverse member record.
- Restrict evidence collection and access to the case, purpose and assigned roles. Record source,
  version, custody and access in the audit trail. Define who may approve preservation, its scope
  and expiry, and review the hold before release, disposal or lawful continued retention. Counsel
  confirms Privacy Act coverage and applicable disclosure and retention limits. [OAIC APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information)
- Have the partner and counsel approve triage, review and update deadlines, an escalation owner,
  and when a claimant receives reasons and can respond through a secure channel. Document any
  lawful notice exception and review it at a set date. A separately authorised human records the
  basis, expiry and review date of any permitted restriction. Continue claim, payout and FW-3
  processes under their existing authority and deadlines unless a lawful restriction applies.
  Confirm who is authorised to investigate and who can decide or settle claims in the selected
  structure. [ASIC claims handling guidance](https://www.asic.gov.au/for-finance-professionals/afs-licensees/apply-for-an-afs-licence/licensing-for-certain-service-providers/claims-handling-and-settling-how-to-comply-with-your-afs-licence-obligations)
- **Gate evidence.** The partner, claims lead and counsel sign the authority and deadline matrix,
  retention rules, and claimant communications before G8. Rehearse a false positive cleared with
  no adverse claim effect, and a referred case through preservation, claimant response, independent
  review and escalation. Verify the audit trail, scope restrictions and available complaint route.

## Out of scope

Dedicated scoring, cross-policy detection and automated evidence correlation are later work.
