# saas-insur

**Insurance as a Service — AI-powered, transparent, and not-for-profit by design.**

## Vision

What if insurance were a service rather than a profit-driven financial product?

Traditional insurance companies generate revenue from premiums and retain underwriting profits when claims are lower than expected. This creates an inherent conflict of financial interests between insurers and policyholders.

**saas-insur** proposes an alternative model in which insurance becomes a software-powered, collectively funded risk management service.

The platform charges a transparent, fixed service fee for its operations. The insurance funds belong collectively to the participants, not the platform.

## How It Works

1. **Submit Data** — Upload historical, statistical and operational data describing the risk you wish to insure.
2. **Analyse Risk** — AI-powered analysis and statistical modelling evaluate the data, estimate probabilities and calculate potential losses.
3. **Design Coverage** — The platform determines:
   - Probability and frequency of claims.
   - Minimum required premiums.
   - Claim payout amounts.
   - Required financial reserves.
   - Risk exposure and confidence intervals.
   - Minimum funding and replenishment thresholds.
4. **Establish a Fund** — Participants contribute to a dedicated insurance fund that is financially separate from the platform.
5. **Process Claims** — Eligible claims are assessed and paid from the fund.
6. **Monitor and Recalculate** — The platform continuously evaluates the fund's financial health, risk exposure and funding requirements.
7. **Replenish When Necessary** — Additional contributions are collected when required to maintain adequate reserves.

## A Fundamentally Different Financial Model

In traditional insurance, premiums are generally recurring payments regardless of whether claims have been made.

With saas-insur, unused contributions remain in the participants' fund.

If few or no claims are made, the fund retains its balance. Participants may not need to make additional contributions until the balance falls below a predefined funding threshold.

The platform earns no underwriting profit and has no financial incentive to reject legitimate claims.

Its operating revenue comes from a fixed, transparent service fee, independent of claim outcomes and unused insurance funds.

## Core Principles

- **Participant-owned funds** — Insurance reserves do not belong to the platform.
- **Not-for-profit risk pooling** — Unused premiums are not converted into platform profits.
- **Transparent pricing** — Premiums and funding requirements are supported by explainable calculations.
- **Fixed service fees** — No percentage-based commissions or profit margins embedded in premiums.
- **Dynamic funding** — Contributions reflect actual funding requirements rather than arbitrary billing cycles.
- **AI-powered risk modelling** — Combine artificial intelligence, actuarial science, statistical modelling and simulation.
- **Explainability** — Participants should understand how their risks, premiums and payouts are calculated.
- **Human oversight** — High-impact decisions remain subject to appropriate review and governance.
- **Open source** — Anyone can inspect, improve, extend or commercially implement the software.

## Potential Applications

The platform aims to be industry-agnostic and could support:

- Community-based insurance funds.
- Small business risk-sharing pools.
- Equipment and machinery coverage.
- Agricultural risk protection.
- Professional associations.
- Employee protection funds.
- Industry-specific mutual insurance.
- Corporate self-insurance.
- Emerging risks that traditional insurers struggle to price.

## Technical Vision

The platform could incorporate:

- AI-powered data ingestion and analysis.
- Automated risk assessment.
- Actuarial modelling.
- Monte Carlo simulations.
- Probability distributions and predictive analytics.
- Dynamic premium calculation.
- Reserve adequacy monitoring.
- Automated fund replenishment recommendations.
- Claims assessment and management.
- Financial ledgers and audit trails.
- Risk modelling APIs.
- Real-time financial transparency dashboards.

AI should assist with risk modelling and decision-making, while validated mathematical and actuarial models provide the foundation for financial calculations.

## What saas-insur Is Not

- A traditional profit-driven insurance company.
- A platform that profits from unused premiums.
- A platform that benefits financially from rejected claims.
- A cryptocurrency or speculative investment scheme.
- A replacement for sound actuarial science or regulatory oversight.

## Mission

To make insurance a transparent, accessible and sustainable service where the financial benefits of successful risk management remain with the people who fund it.

**The platform sells a service, not risk.**

**When risk is low, participants benefit — not the platform.**

## Regulatory Considerations

Insurance is a regulated financial activity in many jurisdictions. Real-world implementations must address applicable licensing, capital adequacy, solvency, fund custody, consumer protection, privacy and regulatory requirements.

This repository provides an open-source technological foundation, not a licensed insurance product or a guarantee of financial viability.
